About VardrSec

Security consulting designed to be direct, practical, and engineering-compatible.

Who we are

VardrSec is a cybersecurity consulting practice focused on offensive security testing, cloud security posture assessment, and security engineering support. We prioritize high-signal findings, realistic threat models, and remediation guidance that engineering teams can implement.

Our work spans web applications, APIs, cloud infrastructure, identity systems, and security controls—anywhere real risk exists and technical precision matters.

Specializations: Application security • Cloud security • API testing • Authorization flaws • Detection engineering
Leadership

Jorge Aquino — Founder & Principal Consultant

Jorge founded VardrSec to bring the discipline and mission focus learned in high-accountability environments to cybersecurity consulting. As a Marine Corps veteran and former Firefighter/EMT, he developed an approach to security that prioritizes clear assessment, evidence-based decision-making, and effective execution under pressure.

His focus areas include offensive security testing, cloud security architecture, and application security—with particular expertise in authorization vulnerabilities, API security patterns, and real-world attacker techniques. Jorge approaches every engagement with an engineering mindset: understanding how systems are designed, how they fail under stress, and how those failures translate into measurable business risk.

His work emphasizes evidence-based findings, practical remediation guidance, and solutions that security and engineering teams can actually implement and sustain.

Professional standard: All security testing is performed with explicit written authorization and documented scope. No unauthorized activity, no ambiguity, no surprises.

Mission & Vision

Our commitment to protecting organizations through disciplined, evidence-based security work.

Mission

Protect what matters through actionable security

VardrSec exists to help organizations identify and remediate real security risks before they become incidents. We approach every engagement with the discipline, precision, and mission focus developed in high-accountability environments—where clear thinking under pressure and attention to detail aren't optional.

Security should enable your business, not slow it down. Our mission is to deliver findings you can act on, guidance you can implement, and results you can measure—without the noise or unnecessary complexity that often accompanies security consulting.

Vision

Security that engineering teams trust and adopt

We envision a security practice where technical teams view security work as a partnership rather than an obstacle. Where findings come with context, remediation guidance is specific and validated, and security measures are designed to be sustainable.

Organizations should have access to practical security expertise that respects their constraints, understands their risk tolerance, and delivers proportional solutions. Security consulting should be measured by outcomes—reduced risk, faster response, and teams that ship with confidence.

Core Values

The principles that guide every engagement and decision.

Integrity and Trust

Operating with absolute honesty, ethics, and discretion. As consultants entrusted with sensitive data and vulnerability information, we maintain strict confidentiality and transparency about risks—even when the news is difficult to deliver.

Relentless Innovation

Staying ahead of evolving threats through continuous research, new methodologies, and modern analytics. We challenge the status quo to provide cutting-edge defense rather than just compliance-driven security.

Client-Centricity

Treating each client's security posture as a personal mission. We focus on tailored solutions aligned with business goals and risk appetite, building long-term collaborative partnerships rather than transactional engagements.

Continuous Learning

Cultivating ongoing education to keep pace with the rapidly changing cyber landscape. We invest in continuous skill development, certifications, and research to ensure our expertise remains current and relevant.

Accountability and Excellence

Taking ownership of outcomes and adhering to the highest quality standards in service delivery. We hold ourselves responsible for our actions, learn from mistakes, and consistently provide reliable, high-quality deliverables.
