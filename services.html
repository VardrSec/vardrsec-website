Services

Typical engagement flow: Short discovery call → scoped statement of work → execution with weekly status updates → final deliverables (findings, remediation guidance, and prioritized roadmap). View engagement models

Standard Deliverables

Executive Summary

High-level risk overview, critical findings, and business impact assessment presented in non-technical terms.

Technical Findings Report

Detailed reproduction steps, evidence, severity ratings, affected systems, and specific remediation guidance.

Prioritized Remediation Roadmap

Fix-first ordering based on risk, quick wins identification, and medium/long-term improvement recommendations.

Professional Standards

Authorization: All security testing is performed only with explicit written authorization and clearly defined scope. No unauthorized or "grey area" testing activities.

Confidentiality: All engagement details, findings, and client information are treated as strictly confidential and protected under NDA.

Responsible Disclosure: Findings are reported directly to clients first, with appropriate time for remediation before any public disclosure.
