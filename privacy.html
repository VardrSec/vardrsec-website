Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Last updated: January 2026

Summary

  • We collect only the information needed to respond to inquiries and deliver services.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We use reasonable safeguards to protect data.

Information we collect

Information you provide

When you contact VardrSec, you may provide your name, email address, company name, and project details. If you engage services, you may provide technical details necessary to perform the work.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, basic technical data may be collected by hosting/CDN providers (e.g., IP address, user agent, timestamps) for security, performance, and troubleshooting.

How information is used

  • To respond to requests and provide services
  • To improve site reliability and security
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website or communications (e.g., hosting, email). These providers are used only as needed to provide the service. We do not sell or share personal information for marketing purposes.

Data retention

Inquiry data is retained only as long as necessary for communication and business purposes. Engagement data is retained based on contractual needs and legal requirements.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Your choices

  • You can request deletion of inquiry emails unless retention is legally required.
  • You can opt out of non-essential communications (if any are used).

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to exercise your privacy rights, contact: VardrSec Contact Form
