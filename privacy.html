Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

Last updated: July 2026

Summary

  • VardrSec collects only what is needed to respond to inquiries and deliver services.
  • VardrSec does not sell personal information.
  • There is no advertising, analytics profiling, or third-party tracking on this site.
  • Reasonable safeguards are used to protect data.

Information collected

Information you provide

When you contact VardrSec, you may provide your name, email address, company name, and project details. If you engage services, you may provide technical details necessary to perform the work.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, basic technical data may be collected by hosting/CDN providers (e.g., IP address, user agent, timestamps) for security, performance, and troubleshooting.

Contact form

Submitting the contact form sends your email address, your message, and your IP address to the processors listed below. Nothing from the form is stored in a database; it is relayed to a mailbox and kept only as email.

Processors

VardrSec uses the following third parties to operate this site. Each receives only what its function requires.

Processor Purpose Data it receives
Cloudflare Hosting, CDN, DNS, email routing IP address, user agent, request metadata; email sent to contact@vardrsec.com
Cloudflare Turnstile Bot check on the contact form IP address and browser signals. No tracking cookie is set for advertising purposes.
Resend Delivering contact form submissions by email Your email address and message content
Cloudflare and Resend are both US-based. Submitting the form means your message is processed in the United States.

How information is used

  • To respond to requests and provide services
  • To improve site reliability and security
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data sharing

Information is shared only with the processors listed above, and only to the extent each needs it to function. VardrSec does not sell personal information and does not share it for marketing purposes. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Data retention

Inquiry data is retained only as long as necessary for communication and business purposes. Engagement data is retained based on contractual needs and legal requirements.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Your choices

  • You can request deletion of inquiry emails unless retention is legally required.
  • You can opt out of non-essential communications (if any are used).

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to exercise your privacy rights, email contact@vardrsec.com or use the contact form.
